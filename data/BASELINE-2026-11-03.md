## Indicators to track going forward (per state)
1. Unemployment rate — lower = improving
2. Payroll job growth (YoY % and jobs) — higher = improving
3. Real GDP growth by state (quarterly) — higher = improving
4. Wages / personal income growth vs inflation — real gain = improving
5. Inflation pressure proxies by state: gas price, housing cost — lower/falling = improving
6. Housing: median home price + price-to-income / salary needed — falling burden = improving
7. New business applications (Census BFS, monthly) — higher = improving
8. Labor force participation, poverty rate (annual Census) — LFPR up / poverty down = improving

## Purpose rule for tracker (owner, 2026-11-05)
The site is for how things compare to the BASELINE — not to historical data.
Every display is Current vs Election Day Baseline: baseline value | current value | change since baseline | color from that change.
No pre-baseline trends, historical charts, or year-ago headlines as content.
Also: this is to become a public "real time updated" webpage — "real time" honestly means latest released official data, updated daily as releases land (BLS/BEA/Census monthly/quarterly, AAA gas daily), with a visible Last Updated stamp.

## Visual rule for tracker (owner, corrected 2026-11-05)
NOT RED — no red anywhere in the tracker, for any purpose.
- GREEN = improving
- YELLOW = mixed / flat
- ORANGE = worsening (not red)
- GREY = no new data since baseline yet
Visual first (map + dots), numbers on detail.

## Scoring rule for tracker
Each indicator compared to its Election Day baseline (above), not to last month alone.
Per state: count improving vs worsening indicators → Overall: Improving / Mixed / Worsening / No new data yet.
Flipped states are compared both to their own baseline AND to the national change and to hold-state average over the same period, so national trends don't get credited/blamed to a new governor.
Caveat: new governors take office Jan 2027 — movements before then are baseline/context, not their record.
